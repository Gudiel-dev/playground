Favourite Books

Project Hail Mary

Author: Andy Weir

Genre: Hard Science Fiction

The Premise

An astronaut wakes up alone aboard a spacecraft with no memory.

Why I Recommend It

  • Clever scientific problem solving.
  • Excellent pacing.
  • Memorable first-contact story.

The Martian

Author: Andy Weir

Genre: Hard Science Fiction

The Premise

An astronaut is accidentally left behind on Mars and must rely on science, creativity, and determination to survive until rescue becomes possible.

Why I Recommend It

  • Brilliant use of real science.
  • Funny and optimistic despite the circumstances.
  • One of the best survival stories I’ve read.

The Will of the Many

Author: James Islington

Genre: Epic Fantasy

The Premise

A gifted young man hides his true identity while infiltrating the empire’s most prestigious academy, where power itself is drawn from the will of others.

Why I Recommend It

  • Outstanding worldbuilding.
  • One of my favorite modern fantasy novels.
  • Political intrigue that keeps you guessing.

Foundation

Author: Isaac Asimov

Genre: Science Fiction

The Premise

As a vast galactic empire approaches collapse, a mathematician develops a plan to preserve civilization through the coming dark age.

Why I Recommend It

  • One of the pillars of science fiction.
  • Fascinating ideas about history and society.
  • Ambitious in both scale and imagination.

Siddhartha

Author: Hermann Hesse

Genre: Philosophical Fiction

The Premise

A young man leaves behind comfort and certainty to seek wisdom, meaning, and inner peace through his own experiences.

Why I Recommend It

  • Beautifully written.
  • Encourages reflection rather than providing answers.
  • A timeless book that rewards rereading.
